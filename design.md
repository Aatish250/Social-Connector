# Project Design Specifications: Simple Social Connector

This document contains visual design DNA, color palettes, and UI/UX patterns extracted from the **Social Connector** project. Use these details to ensure consistency in your presentation (.ppt).

---

## 1. Design Concept & Theme
*   **Aesthetic:** "Velvet Nocturne" (Modern Dark Mode).
*   **Vibe:** Sleek, high-contrast, premium feel with vibrant accents and glassmorphism.
*   **Key Design Keywords:** Dark, Ambient, Fluid, Minimalist, Glassmorphic.

---

## 2. Color Palette (Hex Codes)
Use these exact hex codes in your PPT to match the application:

### A. Primary & Accents (The Brand)
*   **Primary Purple:** `#ac8aff` (Main brand color, used for buttons and active states).
*   **Deep Violet:** `#8455ef` (Used for gradients and secondary emphasis).
*   **Error/Alert:** `#fd6f85` (Soft coral-pink for errors or delete actions).

### B. Neutrals & Surfaces (The Base)
*   **Deep Background:** `#0d0d15` (Main app background).
*   **Surface Low:** `#12121c` (Cards and panels).
*   **Surface High:** `#1e1e2c` (Hover states and elevated panels).
*   **On-Surface (Text):** `#e6e3f8` (Main text color - a soft blueish-white).

### C. Signature Gradients
*   **Primary Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #ac8aff 0%, #8455ef 100%)`
    *   *Usage: Primary buttons, branding, or feature headers.*

---

## 3. Typography
The typography uses a dual-font system for modern hierarchy:

*   **Headlines (Manrope):**
    *   *Font Family:* 'Manrope', sans-serif.
    *   *Usage:* Title slides, section headers, user names.
    *   *Vibe:* Geometric and bold.
*   **Body & Labels (Inter):**
    *   *Font Family:* 'Inter', sans-serif.
    *   *Usage:* Paragraphs, message bubbles, button labels.
    *   *Vibe:* Clean and professional.

---

## 4. UI Patterns & Visual Effects
Incorporate these styles into your PPT slide shapes:

*   **Glassmorphism:**
    *   *Style:* `rgba(18, 18, 26, 0.4)` background with `16px` backdrop-blur.
    *   *Border:* `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
*   **Rounded Corners:**
    *   *Standard:* `0.5rem` (8px).
    *   *Large Panels:* `0.75rem` (12px).
    *   *Pill Shapes:* Full (`9999px`).
*   **Shadows:**
    *   *Ambient Glow:* Subtle dark shadows `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` to create depth on dark backgrounds.
*   **Icons:**
    *   *Library:* Material Symbols Outlined.
    *   *Weight:* 400.

---

## 5. Layout Structure
*   **The 3-Pane Shell:** 
    1.  **Slim Sidebar:** Navigation (left).
    2.  **Contact/List Pane:** Conversation heads (center-left).
    3.  **Active Chat Pane:** Main interaction area (main).
*   **Responsive Flow:** The design uses a flexible grid that expands for desktop but maintains core focus on interaction panes.
